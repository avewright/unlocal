  • Priced before it runs

    Every change shows its monthly cost first. Nothing is created until the plan is approved.

  • One cap for everything

    All your projects share one monthly limit. Raising it takes you, at a keyboard.

  • Built for agents

    One JSON answer per command, stable error codes, and no prompts to get stuck on.

Start

$ unlocal init --cloud cloudflare
$ unlocal plan
$ unlocal apply --yes
